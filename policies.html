Before you book

Check-in and check-out

Check-in is from 11:00 AM and check-out is by 10:00 AM on the day of departure.

Early check-in and late check-out are subject to availability and must be requested in advance. If another booking follows yours, we cannot extend your stay. Late check-out without approval may be charged as an additional half day.

Booking confirmation

When you book online, your dates are held for a short time (shown on the booking page) while you pay the advance.

After paying, enter your UPI transaction reference (UTR). Our team verifies the payment in our bank account and then confirms your booking on WhatsApp or email. A booking is confirmed only after this verification; a screenshot alone does not confirm a booking.

You can check your booking status at any time with your Booking ID and the last four digits of your phone number.

Payment terms

  • An advance is paid online by UPI to confirm your dates. The amount for your package or event is shown before you pay.
  • The balance is paid at the resort at check-in, by UPI or cash.
  • Add-ons such as food, decoration, travel or car rental are paid at the resort and do not change your advance.
  • Prices are in Indian Rupees. Rates for weekends (Friday and Saturday nights), festivals and long weekends may be higher than weekday rates.

Cancellation and rescheduling

To cancel or change your booking, message us on WhatsApp or call us with your Booking ID. The date we receive your request is the date used below.

  • 7 days or more before check-in: full refund of the advance, or move it to new dates free of charge.
  • 3 to 6 days before check-in: 50% of the advance is refunded, or the full advance can be moved to new dates within 3 months.
  • Less than 72 hours before check-in, or no-show: the advance is not refundable.
  • Rescheduling is allowed once per booking and is subject to the new dates being available. If the new dates cost more, the difference is payable.
  • For weddings and large events, decoration and vendor costs already incurred on your behalf are not refundable.

Refunds

Approved refunds are sent to the same UPI account or bank account the payment came from, within 7 working days of approval.

If you paid but your dates were taken before we could confirm, we refund the full advance. If the resort has to cancel your booking for reasons within our control, you receive a full refund or new dates of your choice.

Guest ID

All adult guests must show a valid government photo ID at check-in: Aadhaar card, passport, driving licence or voter ID. PAN cards are not accepted as address proof.

The guest who made the booking must be at least 18 years old and present at check-in. We do not collect ID documents online. The resort may refuse check-in if valid ID is not produced.

Occupancy and visitors

The number of overnight guests must not exceed the limit shown for your package. Extra guests beyond the booking may be charged or refused.

Day visitors are welcome for events and celebrations booked with us, and must leave by 10:00 PM unless they are part of the overnight booking. Children under 5 stay free and are not counted towards the guest limit.

Guest conduct and property use

The main guest is responsible for the conduct of the whole group, including visitors.

  • Smoking is not allowed inside the rooms or the penthouse; please use the open areas.
  • Alcohol may be consumed responsibly by guests aged 21 and above. Drunk or disorderly behaviour is not tolerated.
  • Illegal substances, gambling, fireworks without permission and any illegal activity are strictly prohibited and will lead to immediate eviction without refund.
  • Please be courteous to our staff and to our neighbours.

Damage to property

Please report any damage or breakage as soon as it happens. Damage to the property, furniture, fittings, pool or turf caused by guests or their visitors is charged at the cost of repair or replacement.

For events and large groups, a refundable security deposit may be collected at check-in. It is returned at check-out after the property is inspected, less any damage charges.

Swimming pool safety

  • The pool is open from 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM. Swimming after dark is not allowed.
  • There is no lifeguard on duty. Guests swim at their own risk.
  • Children must be supervised by an adult at all times in and around the pool.
  • Proper swimwear is required. Please shower before entering the pool.
  • No glass, food or alcohol in or at the edge of the pool. Do not swim after drinking.

Amenities and turf rules

The cricket turf, gardens, pool and penthouse are for your group's private use during your stay. The turf can be used until 10:00 PM; floodlights are switched off after that.

Please use sports equipment and outdoor furniture with care and return them after use. Children must be supervised on the turf and in the gardens.

Food and outside food

Food at the resort is served by our partner, Aroma Restaurant. You can choose a food package or order from the Aroma menu, and we will arrange delivery to the resort.

Guests may bring their own snacks and home-cooked food for a stay. For events, outside caterers must be approved by us in advance and may carry a service fee. The kitchen must be left clean after use.

Pets

Pets are not allowed unless approved in writing before your booking. If approved, pets must be kept on a leash outdoors, kept out of the pool, and the owner is responsible for any cleaning or damage.

Noise

As required by law, loud music and loudspeakers outdoors must stop by 10:00 PM. After that, please keep music indoors and at a low volume so the neighbours are not disturbed.

Repeated complaints may lead to the music being switched off by our staff.

Events

  • All events need an event booking with the expected guest count, date and timings agreed in advance.
  • Outdoor events and music must end by 10:00 PM.
  • Decorators, DJs, caterers and other vendors must be approved by us. No nails, tape or adhesives on walls; decoration must be removed by the vendor after the event.
  • Fireworks, sky lanterns and open flames need prior permission.
  • The person booking is responsible for the safety and conduct of all event guests and vendors.

Safety and emergencies

First-aid kits and fire extinguishers are available on the property; ask our caretaker for their location. In an emergency, call the resort number given in your booking confirmation, or dial 112 for emergency services.

Please do not leave children unattended near the pool or the turf, and follow any safety instructions from our staff.

Privacy

When you book or send an enquiry we collect your name, phone number, email (if given), booking details and your UPI transaction reference.

We use these details only to manage your booking, verify your payment and contact you about your stay. Booking alerts go to our booking team on a private messaging group. We never sell or share your details for marketing, and we do not collect ID documents online.

You can ask us to correct or delete your details at any time; booking records needed for accounting are kept for up to 3 years.

Liability

Guests use the pool, turf and all facilities at their own risk. The resort is not responsible for injury caused by misuse of facilities or by not following these policies.

Please keep valuables with you. The resort is not responsible for loss of or damage to personal belongings, vehicles or valuables left on the property.

Terms

By making a booking you agree to these policies. We may update them from time to time; the version shown on this page on the date of your booking applies to that booking.

The document you receive after booking is a booking confirmation, not a tax invoice. The resort may refuse or end a stay, without refund, if these policies are seriously broken.

House rules

  • Check-in 11:00 AM, check-out 10:00 AM.
  • Only registered guests stay overnight.
  • Outdoor music ends by 10:00 PM.
  • No smoking inside the rooms or the penthouse.
  • Pool hours 7:00 AM to 8:00 PM; children always with an adult.
  • Switch off lights, fans and air conditioning when you go out.
  • Leave the property as you found it.

Questions about these policies

If you have a question about any of these policies, message us on WhatsApp or call us before you book. We are happy to explain.
